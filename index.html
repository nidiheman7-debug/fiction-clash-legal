What we collect

Account details: your email (or Google sign-in details), username, display name, profile picture and banner.

What you create: matchups, votes, likes, posts, comments, club and direct messages, clips you share, pictures you upload, and the points and badges you earn.

Device details: a push-notification token so we can send you alerts, and basic activity status (for example "active now"), which you can turn off in Settings.

How we use it

To run the app: sign you in, show your content to other people, count votes, rank the leaderboard, deliver messages and notifications, keep the community safe, and fix problems.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not show ads.

Who helps us run the app

We use trusted services to operate Fiction Clash: Supabase (accounts, database and file storage), Expo and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (push notifications), GIPHY (GIF search, which receives your search words), and YouTube (video links and playback).

These providers only get what they need to do their job and have their own privacy policies.

Your username, display name, picture, banner, points, rank and anything you post are visible to other users. Direct messages are visible to the people in the conversation. Club messages are visible to club members.

We keep your data while your account is active. Ask us to delete your account and personal data at fictionclash6@gmail.com and we will do so, except where we must keep something for safety or legal reasons. Saved copies on your phone are removed when you sign out or uninstall the app.

Content Policy

Last updated October 12, 2026

Pictures and artwork

Fiction Clash does not provide official character art. Pictures come from users. Only upload an image if it is your own artwork, you have the creator's permission, or it is shared for personal, non-commercial fan use.

Do not post official posters, promotional art, logos, screenshots or full copies of episodes, films, books or articles that you do not have the right to share. Link to them or describe them in your own words instead.

Not allowed

Sexual content, and anything sexual involving minors (zero tolerance).

Hate, harassment, threats, bullying, or targeting people for who they are.

Graphic violence or gore, and content that promotes self-harm.

Sharing private information about others, impersonation, scams, spam and malware.

Anything illegal, and copying other people's work as your own.

Matchups and votes

Matchups must be between fictional characters and must be your own idea. Duplicates, joke spam and offensive matchups are declined. To keep the Live deck fresh, only a limited number of matchups can wait in the queue (currently 5); new ones are declined when it is full.

Do not use extra accounts or bots to push votes, likes or points.

Review and removal

Posts and matchups pass an automatic review before they show. If something is declined you will be told. We may remove content or suspend accounts that break this policy, and repeat infringers lose access.

Reporting

Tap the flag on any matchup, post, clip or comment to report it. Rights holders can send a takedown request to fictionclash6@gmail.com.
